Failures and limitations
1. Original alpha_smoke.gd -- packets fails the strict seen > prior_seen assertion.
   Diagnostic copy prints correct updated port 32101, LuckyOtter username, count 2,
   preferred LAN address, and one listing, but seen equals prior_seen in the same
   millisecond. A diagnostic copy adding OS.delay_msec(2) after prior_seen passes
   the entire packet test. Original test and production discovery are unchanged.
   Classify the original test as failed, with a demonstrated timing-sensitive
   assertion; do not silently count the diagnostic result as its replacement.
2. combined_smoke.gd -- local exits 1: configured startup is lobby.tscn, whereas
   that test requires application.tscn. application.tscn is absent.
3. combat_smoke.gd -- host errors at line 26 accessing app.lobby on the old
   lobby Control; its process does not terminate itself and was killed by the
   runner after the five-second bound. Guest/combat simulation was not reached.
4. Direct check of client_waiting_room.gd reports parse/compile errors for missing
   brick/forest_tree/tank/turret SVGs and player/bullet/brick/moose/game/sound
   scripts, with dependent compilation failures. Godot returned exit 0 despite
   these errors; this check is FAILED based on diagnostics.
5. Normal startup loads lobby.tscn, whose room_scene is game_room.tscn. There is
   no accessible Ready/Start/tank combat flow here. Shooting, ice destruction and
   moose contact cannot be accepted as working in this checkout.
6. Health/damage, scoring, victory and match reset remain unimplemented/deferred.
7. Physical two-device LAN, abrupt Wi-Fi loss, latency/loss tolerance, human mouse
   input, graphical appearance/audio and graphical performance were not tested.
